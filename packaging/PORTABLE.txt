KIDSROOM - WINDOWS 10/11 64-BIT

CHAY NGAY (khong can cai Python):
1. Giai nen toan bo ZIP ra mot thu muc.
2. Nhan dup Kidsroom.exe.
3. Cha me dat mat khau va them de bai.
4. Muon thoat: Cha me - Quan ly -> Thoat giao dien.

KHONG tach file EXE khoi thu muc _internal.
Ban chay truc tiep chua co service tu khoi dong khi Windows dang nhap.

CAI SERVICE (tuy chon, can Administrator):
Mo PowerShell Run as Administrator tai thu muc nay, chay:
.\Install-Service.ps1 -ChildAccount "$env:COMPUTERNAME\hocsinh"
Thay hocsinh bang ten tai khoan Windows cua tre.
Script sao chep vao Program Files va cai service, nhung CHUA khoi dong service.
Thiet lap trong tai khoan tre truoc khi reboot, sau do: Start-Service Kidsroom

DUNG / GO SERVICE:
Stop-Service Kidsroom
Vao quan ly trong GUI, thoat giao dien, sau do:
& "C:\Program Files\Kidsroom\KidsroomService.exe" remove

Doc README.md de xem gioi han bao ve, sao luu va huong dan chi tiet.
Topmost khong phai kiosk; nen dung tai khoan Standard User rieng cho tre.
Ban EXE chua ky so. Khong tat antivirus/SmartScreen de chay.
